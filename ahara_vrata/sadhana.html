Pranic Foods — Living Energy
Food that is still close to its living state carries more energy and clarity for the body and mind. Foods rich in prana: fresh fruits, vegetables, nuts, sprouted grains, and ash gourd. Sadhguru suggests keeping 40–50% of the daily diet in this "alive" form — not overly cooked, processed, or stored — so the body stays light and the mind stays clear.
