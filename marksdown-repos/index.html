Your 7-Day AI Architect Sprint

Each day is thematically sequenced — foundations first, then retrieval, then agents, then design, infrastructure, security, and finally operations. Every day pairs overview notes (your study guide) with real articles (concrete examples). Don't skip days — each one builds on the last. Aim for 2–3 hours per day. That's enough to go deep without burning out.

Foundations, Transformers & LLM Architecture
Start with the why before the how. Understand attention and transformers before touching any framework. These are the bedrock — every other topic in this plan builds directly on what you learn today.
