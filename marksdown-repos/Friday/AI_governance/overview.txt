# AI Governance & Responsible AI — Notes Overview

Especially critical for senior/VP-level roles and fintech.

Core Governance Concepts:
- AI governance frameworks
- Model governance (who approves, audits, retires models)
- Model risk management
- Explainability — can you explain why the model decided X?
- Transparency — is the AI usage disclosed to end users?
- Auditability — can every decision be traced and reviewed?
- Data lineage — where did the training/retrieval data come from?
- Model lineage — what version of what model produced this output?
- Human oversight mechanisms
- Responsible AI principles

Regulatory Compliance:
- AI risk classification (EU AI Act risk tiers)
- Model approval processes (like MRM in banking)
- GDPR implications for AI (right to explanation)
- SR 11-7 (Federal Reserve guidance on model risk)
- PCI-DSS implications for AI in payment systems
- Data residency and sovereignty

Guardrails Architecture:
AI Application
→ Policy Engine
→ Guardrails (input + output)
→ PII Detection
→ Model
→ Output Validation
→ Audit Log

Guardrails tools:
- NeMo Guardrails (NVIDIA)
- Llama Guard
- AWS Bedrock Guardrails
- Custom policy engines

Fintech-specific governance:
- Model risk committee approval workflows
- Explainability for credit/underwriting decisions
- Fair lending compliance (bias detection)
- Audit trail for every AI-assisted decision
- Model performance degradation monitoring and escalation

Key architect deliverable: Design a governance layer, not just a model layer.
