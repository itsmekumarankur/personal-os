# About Me

This folder contains pages about you. Maintained by the agent, verified by you.

## Pages Here

- `role.md` — Your professional identity and direction
- `goals.md` — Active learning and career goals
- `preferences.md` — How you work, tools, operating rhythm
- `journal/` — Daily reflections, filed by date

## How This Gets Built

The `/setup` command creates and refines these pages from:
- What you tell the agent directly
- Your existing notes in `projects/architect-kit/` and `projects/antarmukh/`
- Anything you drop in `inbox/`

You can also update these anytime by just telling the agent what's changed. It will update the relevant pages and cross-link them into the wiki.
