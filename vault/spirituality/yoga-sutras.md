# Yoga Sutras of Patanjali — Architecture of a Steady Mind

196 sutras in 4 chapters (padas). The entire science of how the mind moves and how to still it.

## The Central Sutra

**Yoga chitta vritti nirodha** — Yoga is the cessation of the movements of the mind.

What remains when the mind is still? The Seer (Drashtu) rests in its own nature. Otherwise, the Seer takes the coloring of the vrittis — mistaking mental activity for who it is.

## The Four Chapters

| Pada | Name | Subject |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Samadhi Pada | The nature of Yoga and states of absorption |
| 2 | Sadhana Pada | The practices — Ashtanga Yoga (eight limbs) |
| 3 | Vibhuti Pada | Powers (siddhis) that arise with mastery |
| 4 | Kaivalya Pada | Liberation — the final condition |

## The Eight Limbs (Ashtanga)

1. **Yama** — ethical restraints (ahimsa, satya, asteya, brahmacharya, aparigraha)
2. **Niyama** — personal observances (saucha, santosha, tapas, svadhyaya, Ishvara pranidhana)
3. **Asana** — stable, comfortable posture
4. **Pranayama** — regulation of breath/prana
5. **Pratyahara** — withdrawal of senses inward
6. **Dharana** — concentration on a single object
7. **Dhyana** — sustained, unbroken flow of concentration
8. **Samadhi** — absorption — the meditator and the object of meditation become one

The outer three (1-3) stabilize the body and life. The inner three (6-8) are Samyama — the direct tools of liberation.

## Chitta Vritti — The Five Movements

The mind (chitta) moves in five ways:
1. Pramana — valid cognition (perception, inference, testimony)
2. Viparyaya — misconception
3. Vikalpa — conceptualization without object (imagination)
4. Nidra — sleep (the mind rests in tamasic vritti)
5. Smriti — memory

Practice stills these. Not by suppressing them — by recognizing the Seer that is prior to them.

## Connection to Shambhavi

Shambhavi Mahamudra works with pratyahara and dharana — the inward turn and the concentration. Samyama is the fruit; Shambhavi plants the seed. The Yoga Sutras give the map; the practice is the territory.
