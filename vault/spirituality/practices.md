## Texts Being Studied

From the [[projects/antarmukh/status]] project:

**Mandukya Upanishad** — Shortest and most philosophically dense of the Upanishads. 12 verses. Deals with the nature of consciousness, the four states (waking/jagrat, dreaming/svapna, deep sleep/sushupti, and turiya — the witness beyond all states), and the meaning of OM as Brahman. The Gaudapada Karika and Shankara's commentary extend it further.

**Yoga Sutras of Patanjali** — 196 sutras. The science of the mind. Defines yoga as "chitta vritti nirodha" (cessation of fluctuations of the mind). The eight limbs (yama, niyama, asana, pranayama, pratyahara, dharana, dhyana, samadhi).
