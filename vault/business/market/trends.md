# Indian Wealth Management — Sector Trends

## Market Size

- Wealth management market: USD 171B (2025) → USD 436B by 2034 (10.6% CAGR)
- Mutual fund AUM: $0.78T (2025) → $1.78T by 2030 (18%+ CAGR)
- Demat accounts: 224.5M+ as of March 2026 (24.4% CAGR since FY2016)
- SIP inflows: ₹30,000+ crore/month, resilient through corrections

## Structural Shifts (2026)

1. **SEBI F&O regulations** — tighter rules on derivative trading hurting volume-dependent brokers (Zerodha, Angel One). Opportunity: platforms with MF/equity focus (like Optimus) less impacted.
2. **AI-first wealth managers** — GenZ/millennial DIY investors driving demand for AI-led features. Goal-based planning, tax optimisation, NRI cross-border advisory becoming standard.
3. **Groww IPO** — founders targeting $1.9B boost. When it happens, expect heavy marketing spend and product acceleration from Groww.
4. **Platform convergence** — INDmoney adding US stocks. Kuvera deepening tax harvesting. Line between broker and wealth manager blurring.
5. **MF household penetration** — up from 3% of household savings (2020) to 13% (2025). Growth runway remains large.

## Optimus Competitive Implications

- F&O regulatory headwinds reduce competitor resources → window to improve MF/demat experience
- AI features are table stakes, not differentiators — need to ship now
- Groww IPO = capital influx → expect pricing changes and new product launches in next 12 months
- G-Sec, insurance, LAMF remain less competitive — maintain depth here
