# Kuvera

## What They Do

Commission-free direct MF platform. Now part of CAMS ecosystem. Founded 2016.

## Current State (2026-08-10)

- **Differentiator**: Tax harvesting (auto-switch to save capital gains), portfolio rebalancing, goal-based planning.
- **Model**: Direct-pay, no commissions. Analytical investor focus.
- **Integration**: CAMS ecosystem gives data depth advantage.

## Overlap with Optimus

Direct MF. Goal-based planning is a Kuvera strength — Optimus should match or exceed.

## Watch

Any expansion into demat/stocks or insurance — would expand their threat surface.
