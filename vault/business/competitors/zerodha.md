# Zerodha

## What They Do

India's largest discount broker. Kite (trading), Coin (MF), Varsity (learning).

## Current State (2026-08-10)

- **Business**: Hurt by SEBI F&O regulations reducing derivative volumes.
- **Pricing**: ₹300/year AMC (free first year). Brokerage 0.03% — lowest among peers for active traders.
- **Products**: Coin for direct MF. No insurance or LAMF.
- **Competitive posture**: Losing revenue to F&O curbs. May accelerate MF/wealth features.

## Overlap with Optimus

MF (via Coin), demat/stocks. Partial overlap.

## Watch

Any new Coin features — if they add G-Sec, insurance, or goal-based planning, direct Optimus threat.
