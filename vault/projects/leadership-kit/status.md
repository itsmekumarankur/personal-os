## The 100-Day Shift

**Vision:** "By Day 100: I proactively lead alignment in meetings through real-time visualization, and I am seen as someone who makes rooms leave clearer than they entered."

### Phase 1 (Days 1-33): Meeting Clarifier & Visualizer

Goal: In any meeting, take initiative to draw, diagram, or document the discussion in real time so everyone leaves aligned.

Weekly progression:
- Week 1: Observe & Sketch — build the visualization reflex
- Week 2: Practice Out Loud — verbal + visual fluency
- Week 3: Live Practice — in real low-stakes meetings
- Week 4: Own the Role — in cross-team/leadership meetings
- Days 31-33: Consolidate, feedback, template library

**30-day success marker:** 3+ real meetings where I proactively drew/documented without being asked, and someone said it helped.

**Non-negotiable:** 10-minute daily micro-habit — take one real problem, explain it on paper with one diagram type.
