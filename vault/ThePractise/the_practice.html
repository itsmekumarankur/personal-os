A Living Code

The Practice

Not laws handed down — practices chosen, and returned to, on purpose. Few rules now. More, as they're earned.

Rule 01

Come Back to the Breath

"Wherever I am, my breath is already there — I only have to notice it."

in … and out … and in …

Whenever a spare moment opens up — waiting, walking, sitting in silence between tasks — bring attention to the small patch of sensation just inside the nostrils: cool air arriving, warm air leaving. No controlling the breath, no counting, no fixing anything. Just watching it move, the way you'd watch a candle flame. It's a rule about noticing you're already alive, right now, in this exact breath.

Practice
  • Any free moment, no schedule needed — the cue is simply "I have nothing to do right now."
  • Attention goes to the nostrils only: the coolness in, the warmth out.
  • When the mind wanders (it will), gently bring it back. That return is the whole practice.
Rule 02

Be a Person People Don't Forget

"Anyone should be able to talk to me — and leave remembering that they did."

warmth, attention, presence

Build the kind of presence that makes conversation easy for the other person: genuinely listening, staying warm and open, showing up fully involved rather than half-there. Learn to read a room and move between registers on purpose — professional and precise in a meeting, easy and human once the meeting relaxes — so people don't just talk to you, they look forward to it.

Daily Practice — 30 Minutes
  • Voice (10 min): record yourself talking — a topic, a recap of your day — then play it back for pace, clarity, and warmth. Say it again, better.
  • Register (10 min): take one real exchange from the day and replay it two ways — once fully professional, once fully friendly. Notice what shifts: word choice, pace, humor.
  • Involvement (10 min): review one conversation from today. Was I actually listening, or waiting to speak? Note one moment I could have leaned in more.
