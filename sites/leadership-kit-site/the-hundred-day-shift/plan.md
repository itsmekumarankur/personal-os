# 100-Day Plan

## Vision

By Day 100: I proactively lead alignment in meetings through real-time visualization, and I am seen as someone who makes rooms leave clearer than they entered.

## Phases

### Phase 1 — Days 1–33 · Meeting Clarifier & Visualizer
**Skill:** In any meeting — even when not the main speaker — take initiative to draw, diagram, or document the discussion in real time so everyone leaves aligned.

- [ ] Week 1 (Days 1–7): Observe & Sketch — build the visualization reflex for myself
- [ ] Week 2 (Days 8–14): Practice Out Loud — verbal + visual fluency in solo rehearsal
- [ ] Week 3 (Days 15–21): Live Practice — apply in real low-stakes team meetings
- [ ] Week 4 (Days 22–30): Own the Role — step up in cross-team / leadership meetings
- [ ] Days 31–33: Consolidate — self-review, peer feedback, reusable template library

**30-day success marker:** In at least 3 real meetings this month, I proactively drew or documented the discussion without being asked, and someone told me it helped.

### Phase 3 — Days 67–100

## Non-negotiables

- 10-minute daily micro-habit: take one real work problem, explain it on paper using one diagram type (flow, table, or box)
- Weekly review every Sunday: reflect on the tracker log, adjust the next week's approach

## Weekly review cadence

Every 7 days: reflect on the progress tracker (`meeting-clarifier-progress.md`), adjust, recommit.
