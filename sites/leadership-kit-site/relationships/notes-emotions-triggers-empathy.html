              EMPATHY
                 +
             BOUNDARIES
                 │
                 ▼
       STRONG RELATIONSHIP

Example:

"I understand you're upset
and I want to help.

BUT

I can't continue this conversation
if we're shouting."

So:

EMPATHY
  +
FIRMNESS
  =
EMOTIONAL MATURITY

     │
     ▼

     │
     ▼

     │
     ▼

SETS THE EMOTIONAL CLIMATE
