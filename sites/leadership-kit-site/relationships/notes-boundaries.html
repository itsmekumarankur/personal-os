Boundaries

What they are, healthy vs unhealthy, workplace & personal examples, warning signs, and how to build them

1 What Are Boundaries? ⭐⭐⭐

                    BOUNDARIES
                         │
                         ▼
              CLEAR GUIDELINES ABOUT
                 WHAT IS ACCEPTABLE
                         │
              ┌──────────┼──────────┐
              │          │          │
              ▼          ▼          ▼
            TIME       ENERGY     BEHAVIOR
              │          │          │
              ▼          ▼          ▼
          "When can    "What can   "How can
           you reach    you expect   you treat
           me?"         from me?"    me?"

Boundaries are NOT:

BOUNDARIES
    │
    ├── ≠ WALLS
    ├── ≠ PUNISHMENT
    ├── ≠ REJECTION
    ├── ≠ AGGRESSION
    └── ≠ SELFISHNESS

Instead:

BOUNDARIES
    =
CLARITY + SELF-RESPECT + PROTECTION

2 Boundary = Emotional Property Line ⭐⭐⭐

Think about your personal space like property:

             MY SPACE                    YOUR SPACE
        ┌─────────────────┐          ┌─────────────────┐
        │                 │          │                 │
        │   MY TIME       │          │   YOUR TIME     │
        │   MY ENERGY     │          │   YOUR ENERGY   │
        │   MY VALUES     │          │   YOUR VALUES   │
        │   MY CHOICES    │          │   YOUR CHOICES  │
        │                 │          │                 │
        └────────┬────────┘          └────────┬────────┘
                 │                            │
                 └──────── BOUNDARY ──────────┘

The boundary says:

        "THIS IS WHERE
          I END

           AND

        YOU BEGIN."

6 Boundary Violations Accumulate ⭐⭐⭐

This is a very important concept.

Violation #1
     ↓
You say nothing

Violation #2
     ↓
You say nothing

Violation #3
     ↓
You say nothing

Violation #4
     ↓
You say nothing

Violation #5
     ↓
💥 EXPLOSION

The explosion is usually not about the fifth event alone.

5th event
   +
previous 4 violations
   =
ACCUMULATED RESENTMENT

Better:

FIRST VIOLATION
      ↓
CALM BOUNDARY
      ↓
CLEAR EXPECTATION
      ↓
PROBLEM STOPPED EARLY

20 How to Communicate a Boundary ⭐⭐⭐

The pattern should be:

           BOUNDARY
              │
              ▼
        CALM + CLEAR
              │
              ▼
          SPECIFIC
              │
              ▼
         NO ATTACK
              │
              ▼
        NO OVER-EXPLAINING

Examples:

Instead of:

"You ALWAYS interrupt me!"

Say:

"I'd like to finish my point
before we move on."
Instead of:

"I guess it's fine..."

Say:

"I'm not available for that."
Instead of:

"Sorry, sorry, sorry...
I can't..."

Say:

"I can't commit to that."

CLEAR BOUNDARY
