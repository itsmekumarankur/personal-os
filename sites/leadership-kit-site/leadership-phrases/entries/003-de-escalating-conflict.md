scenario: De-escalating a tense cross-team meeting in real time
category: Conflict

> "Let's separate the decision from the delivery — I think we agree on more than it feels like right now."

> "Can we park the 'who' for a second and get aligned on the 'what'?"

**Why it works:** Names the emotional temperature without calling it out directly, and redirects the room toward the shared goal instead of the disagreement.
